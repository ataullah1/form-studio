# Asset credits

## Wood materials

Oak, walnut and ash maps are by **Jenelle van Heerden**, distributed by Poly Haven under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

| Finish | Source | Local maps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oak | [Oak Veneer 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_02) | `public/assets/textures/wood/oak/` |
| Walnut | [Black Walnut Veneer 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/black_walnut_veneer_01) | `public/assets/textures/wood/walnut/` |
| Ash | [Ash Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/ash_veneer) | `public/assets/textures/wood/ash/` |

Each set contains colour, OpenGL normal and roughness WebP maps, converted from the source 1K JPEGs. Colour is sRGB; normal and roughness are data maps. The app applies concept finish coats and metre-based UV tiling. These are not measured manufacturer samples. Source URLs, authors, licenses and hashes are retained in [texture-sources.json](texture-sources.json).

## Project artwork and geometry

The hero image, three accessory images, README presentation image and social banner artwork were supplied by the repository owner. They are concept visuals, not photographs of a manufactured product. The social PNG is a resized, palette-optimized derivative of the supplied banner. No external source or additional license was provided for the supplied artwork.

The parameterized desk, accessories, room geometry, dimension guides, SVG fallback, material-study illustrations and app monogram are authored in project code. Model PNG downloads capture the current scene and existing licensed material maps.

The third-party licenses above apply to their respective assets; they do not assign a license to the whole repository.
